## What it does

Outbound Sequencer AI writes and adapts multi-touch email/LinkedIn sequences per persona, detects reply intent (meeting, objection, unsubscribe, referral), and routes hot replies to reps instantly.

## Key features

- **Persona messaging** — sequences per ICP, industry and trigger event.
- **Reply-intent AI** — classify & route replies in seconds.
- **Auto-pause on out-of-office** — and resume on return.
- **A/B subject & CTA testing** — with significance tracking.
- **Deliverability guardrails** — domain warmup & throttle rules.

## Who it's for

SDR/BDR teams and founders doing founder-led sales.
